This screen — Tag archive (/tags/{slug})

  • Shows the posts carrying one tag, newest first (§4.3).
  • Reached from the home tag list, a badge in the tag bar, or a badge on a post. The header is the tag's name, its description when it has one, and its post count (§7.5 Tag page).
  • The tag bar stays, with the selected tag inverted (§7.5 Tag bar).
  • Private posts are listed with a Private badge; only the signed-in author can open their body (§5.1).
  • Old /categories/{slug}, /series/{slug} and /collections/{slug} addresses redirect here.
